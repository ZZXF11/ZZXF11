### 🔬 About me

I am a Ph.D. student at **South China University of Technology**, advised by [Prof. Lianwen Jin](http://dlvc-lab.net/lianwen/) at the [Deep Learning and Vision Computing Lab](https://github.com/SCUT-DLVCLab).

My research spans **document intelligence, computer vision, and multimodal learning**, with a current focus on:

- **Multimodal OCR** — large models for reading and understanding complex visual documents.
- **AIGC** — visual text generation, editing, and document content creation.
- **Document restoration** — recovering historical text and its visual appearance.
